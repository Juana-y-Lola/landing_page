## The idea

A neighborhood café needs its website to feel as welcoming as the place itself. This concept brings the essentials together on one page: an introduction, menu highlights, opening hours, and a clear invitation to visit.

## What it could include

- A visual introduction that conveys the café’s character.
- A menu and updates that are easy to browse on a phone.
- Opening hours, location, and contact details that are easy to find.

This is an illustrative concept, not a real business project.
