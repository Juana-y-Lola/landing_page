## The idea

A small appointment-based studio needs a clear experience for clients and an organized view for the person running the day-to-day work. This concept connects a public booking site with a private dashboard.

## What it could include

- Services and availability presented simply.
- A calendar for reviewing and organizing bookings.
- A dashboard for updating hours and managing appointments.

This is an illustrative concept, not a real business project.
