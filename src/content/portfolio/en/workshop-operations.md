## The idea

As work grows, scattered notes and spreadsheets can make everyday tasks harder. This concept shows a lightweight tool that helps a small workshop see what is pending, what is moving, and what needs attention.

## What it could include

- Order tracking with clear statuses.
- A shared task list with visible priorities.
- A stock overview to plan purchases ahead.

This is an illustrative concept, not a real business project.
